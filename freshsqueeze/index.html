Before you order

The questions we get most.

How long does a bottle keep?

Three days from the pressed time on the cap, kept cold. Shake before you drink. It separates because nothing is added to stop it.

Why glass?

Glass takes on no flavor and it comes back to us. Rinse it, leave it out on delivery morning, and $1 comes off your next box.

What if I'm not home?

Every box ships in an insulated bag with ice packs good for six hours on a doorstep. Tell us a spot in the shade and we'll use it.

Can I change my mix?

Until 9 p.m. the night before delivery. After that the press list is locked and the fruit is already cut.
