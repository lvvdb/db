## What is the route from Sunny Isles Beach?

Stay on Collins Avenue heading north. It becomes Ocean Boulevard through Golden Beach and South Ocean Drive once you enter Hallandale Beach. At Hallandale Beach Boulevard turn left, cross the bridge over the Intracoastal Waterway, and take a left onto Golden Isles Drive. Number 501 is on that street.

If Collins is slow, the other way is west over the William Lehman Causeway to Biscayne Boulevard, north past Gulfstream Park, then east on Hallandale Beach Boulevard. [[CONFIRM: drive time and distance from Collins Ave at 174th St]]

## Can I handle most of it from my condo?

Much of it. Your first visit and any blood draw happen at the clinic. After that, results reviews and routine follow-ups can often be done on [telehealth](/telehealth), and IV therapy can come to you. Hormone prescriptions have their own rules about in-person visits, which the telehealth page explains.

## How is a local clinic different from an online-only service?

You can meet the person prescribing for you. An online-only company cannot examine you, take your blood pressure or see you the same week if something feels off.
