## What is the best way to reach you from Hollywood?

It depends which part of Hollywood you start in. There are three easy routes, and none involves more than two turns once you reach Hallandale Beach Boulevard.

- **From Downtown and the Lakes:** go south on Federal Highway from Young Circle. Cross Pembroke Road into Hallandale Beach, turn left onto East Hallandale Beach Boulevard and then right onto Golden Isles Drive.
- **From Hollywood Beach:** follow A1A south along Ocean Drive, past the Diplomat. Turn right onto Hallandale Beach Boulevard, cross the Intracoastal bridge and turn left onto Golden Isles Drive.
- **From Hollywood Hills, Emerald Hills and the west side:** take I-95 south to exit 18 and drive east on Hallandale Beach Boulevard.
