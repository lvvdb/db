## How does mobile IV work in Hallandale Beach?

You call, we ask a few health questions, and a licensed professional comes to you with sterile supplies. Before any drip starts, your history, medications and vital signs are checked. The infusion runs for 30 to 60 minutes, you are monitored throughout, and everything is cleared away when we leave.

## What should I arrange with my building?

Register us as a visitor, tell us where guests park and pick a comfortable spot with good light. Most condo buildings here have a front desk, a guard gate or both, so these three steps make the visit smooth:

1. Register us as a visitor before we arrive.
2. Tell us where guests are allowed to park, or whether there is valet.
3. Pick a spot with a chair or sofa near a table and good light.

If your association restricts service visits to certain hours, let us know when you book.
