## Do you offer mobile IV in Sunny Isles Beach?

Yes. Our mobile team travels down A1A from Hallandale Beach, passing through Golden Beach, to reach the towers and hotels on Collins Avenue. [[CONFIRM: mobile service area and fee]]

## How does a visit work in an oceanfront tower?

Sunny Isles Beach is almost all high-rise, so access is the main thing to arrange. Add us to your visitor list, tell us where to park and let us know if the elevator needs a fob. Before the appointment:

- Put our name on your visitor list with the concierge
- Tell us whether to use valet, guest parking or the service entrance
- Let us know your floor and whether the elevator needs a fob

Once we are in your unit we need a seat for you, a surface for supplies and about an hour.

## What does the nurse check first?

Your health history, your medications and allergies, your blood pressure and your pulse. If anything suggests a drip is not safe for you today, we stop there and tell you what we recommend instead. People with heart failure or kidney disease, and anyone who is pregnant, need clearance before an IV.

Not for IV therapy, which is a single visit. If you want ongoing care such as hormone therapy while you are here, see how that works on our [Sunny Isles Beach page](/areas/sunny-isles-beach).

## How do I book?

Call the clinic with your building name, your unit and your medication list. If you feel unwell most weeks, a drip is a short-term fix. A [blood panel](/lab-testing) is the better place to start.
