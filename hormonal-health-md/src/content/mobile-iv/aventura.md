## Do you bring IV therapy to Aventura?

Yes. Aventura is the city directly south of our clinic, a straight run down Biscayne Boulevard. Our team brings the same sterile supplies and the same drip menu we use in the clinic. [[CONFIRM: mobile service area and fee]]

## Where in Aventura do you go?

Most visits are to the condo towers around the Country Club Drive loop and to the gated communities east of Biscayne: Williams Island, Mystic Pointe, Porto Vita and The Waterways. We also come to Turnberry Isle and Aventura Lakes.

If you are near the southern edge of the city around the Lehman Causeway, mention it when you call so we can confirm you are inside the service area.

## How do visits work in a high-rise?

Aventura buildings tend to have a concierge, valet parking and package rules. Tell the front desk a medical visitor is expected and ask where we should park, since some towers send service visitors to a separate entrance. Once we are upstairs, all we need is a comfortable seat and a clear surface.

Every visit begins with a health screening and a check of your blood pressure and pulse. [[CONFIRM: who performs the pre-infusion health screening, their license, and the supervising clinician]]

## What does it cost in Aventura?

You pay for the drip plus a travel fee for coming to you. [[CONFIRM: drip menu and prices]] The drips themselves are described on our [IV therapy page](/treatments/iv-therapy).

Groups are welcome. If several people in your home or building want a drip at the same visit, ask about that when you book. [[CONFIRM: group pricing for mobile IV]]

## Should I just drive to the clinic?

If you have the time, it costs less. Head north on Biscayne Boulevard, turn east at Hallandale Beach Boulevard and south onto Golden Isles Drive. Directions and what to expect are on our [Aventura page](/areas/aventura).

A drip treats dehydration. It does not explain why you are tired every week. If that is what is going on, read [always tired](/conditions/always-tired) and consider [lab testing](/lab-testing) first.

## How do I book a mobile IV in Aventura?

Call the clinic with your address, your building's visitor rules and your medication list. If you have a heart or kidney condition or are pregnant, tell us at the start, because some drips will not be suitable.
