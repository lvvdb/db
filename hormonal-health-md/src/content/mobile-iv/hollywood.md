## Is mobile IV available in Hollywood?

Yes, in the parts of Hollywood we can reach reliably from our clinic. East Hollywood is closest to us: the beach, the Lakes, Downtown, Parkside and Park East. Neighborhoods west of I-95 such as Hollywood Hills and Emerald Hills are further, so availability there depends on the schedule.

## When does a mobile IV make sense in Hollywood?

When getting to a clinic is the hard part. That might be a resident recovering from a stomach bug or a long flight who should not be driving, or a visitor on Hollywood Beach who has had too much sun and needs fluids. Everyone gets the same screening before anything is started.

If you are sick with a high fever, cannot keep any fluids down or feel faint, you need urgent care or an emergency department, not a wellness drip. We will tell you that on the phone.

## What happens when the nurse arrives?

The visit follows a set order. We review your health history and medications, take your blood pressure and pulse, and confirm the drip is suitable. Then the IV is placed and the infusion runs while you rest. Allow about an hour in total.

## Would the clinic be easier?

From east Hollywood, quite possibly. The clinic is one turn off Hallandale Beach Boulevard, just south of the Hollywood line, and coming in means no travel fee. Routes from each part of the city are on our [Hollywood page](/areas/hollywood).

## How do I arrange a visit?

Phone the clinic and tell us where in Hollywood you are staying, how many people want treatment and what medications each person takes. If you keep needing drips to get through the week, ask about [lab testing](/lab-testing) instead.
