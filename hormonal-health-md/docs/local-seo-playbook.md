# Local SEO playbook: Hormonal Health MD

A ready-to-run checklist for the map pack, reviews, listings and local links. Everything here happens off the website, in accounts only the clinic controls. Work top to bottom. Text in code blocks is written to be pasted as is.

Anything marked `[[CONFIRM: ...]]` is a fact only the clinic can supply. Fill those first, because several steps depend on them.

> Research behind this playbook was done on 9 November 2026. Search results and listings drift, so re-check anything older than 90 days.

## 1. Google Business Profile

A large share of "near me" searches end at the three map results. This profile is what ranks there.

### 1.1 Claim and verify

- [ ] Sign in at business.google.com with an account the clinic owns, not a personal account and not an agency's.
- [ ] Search for the clinic. If a profile exists, request ownership. If two exist, do not create a third. Claim one and report the other as a duplicate.
- [ ] Complete verification. Google usually asks for a short video showing the street sign, the building entrance, the suite door and proof you can open it. Film it in one take.
- [ ] Add a second owner so access is never tied to one person.

### 1.2 Core details

- [ ] **Name:** `Hormonal Health MD` and nothing else. No "TRT", no "Hallandale Beach", no "Hormone Clinic". Keywords in the name get profiles suspended.
- [ ] **Address and phone:** exactly as in section 0.
- [ ] **Website:** `https://hormonalhealthmd.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
- [ ] **Appointment link:** `https://hormonalhealthmd.com/assessment` until a booking link exists. `[[CONFIRM: online booking link, if one is offered]]`
- [ ] **Hours:** `[[CONFIRM: opening hours for each day of the week]]`. Add special hours for every public holiday.
- [ ] **Service area:** add the cities the mobile team actually reaches. Start with Hallandale Beach, Aventura, Hollywood and Sunny Isles Beach, then trim or extend to match `[[CONFIRM: mobile service area and fee]]`.

### 1.3 Categories

The primary category is the strongest ranking signal on the profile, so choose it from evidence.

- [ ] Search Google Maps for `hormone therapy Hallandale Beach` from a browser in the area.
- [ ] Note the primary category of each of the top three results. A free category viewer extension shows it, or open each profile and read the grey label under the name.
- [ ] Use the category that at least two of the three share as your primary, provided it truthfully describes the clinic.
- [ ] Add secondary categories for weight loss and for IV therapy, choosing from what Google offers in the picker.
- [ ] Record what you chose and the date here: `[[CONFIRM: primary and secondary categories chosen]]`

Do not choose a specialty category, such as endocrinologist or urologist, unless a clinician with that board certification practices here.

## 2. Reviews

This is the biggest gap and the slowest to close, so it starts on day one. Competitors that rank locally show 110, 250, 261 and 508 Google reviews.

### 2.1 Baseline and targets

- [ ] Record today's numbers: `[[CONFIRM: current Google rating and count]]`
- [ ] Target 1: **100 reviews within six months.** From a low base that is about four new reviews a week.
- [ ] Target 2: **250 reviews** after that.
- [ ] Log the count on the first of every month.

### 2.2 The short link

- [ ] In the Google Business Profile, choose "Ask for reviews" and copy the link. It looks like `https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXX/review`.
- [ ] Give that link to whoever maintains the website. They paste it into `src/data/redirects.json` as the destination for `/review`, replacing the placeholder. `[[CONFIRM: review link]]`
- [ ] Test that `https://hormonalhealthmd.com/review` opens the Google review form.

### 2.3 Front desk card

Print this on a card with a QR code that points to `https://hormonalhealthmd.com/review`.

```
How did we do?
Scan to leave an honest review on Google.
It takes about a minute and helps other people find us.
hormonalhealthmd.com/review
```

### 2.4 The message, sent to every patient

Send it after every visit, to everyone, not only the patients who seemed happy. Text needs prior consent. `[[CONFIRM: consent to text patients]]`

Text message:

```
Hi [First name], thank you for visiting Hormonal Health MD. If you have a minute, we would be grateful for an honest review: https://hormonalhealthmd.com/review  Reply STOP to opt out.
```

Email:

```
Subject: A quick favor from Hormonal Health MD

Hi [First name],

Thank you for coming in. If you have a minute, would you leave us an honest review on Google? It helps other people in the area find care.

https://hormonalhealthmd.com/review

Thank you,
The team at Hormonal Health MD
```

The message never mentions a treatment, a result or a reason for the visit.

### 2.5 Rules that are not optional

- [ ] Ask everyone. Never screen for satisfaction first and send only happy patients to Google. That is review gating and it breaks Google's policy.
- [ ] No discounts, gifts, entries or credits in exchange for a review.
- [ ] No reviews from staff, owners, family or vendors.
- [ ] Never write a review for a patient, even with their permission.
- [ ] Never buy reviews or use a service that promises a number.
- [ ] Do not ask patients to mention keywords or services.

### 2.6 Reply templates

Reply to every review within two working days. A reply must never confirm that the reviewer is a patient or mention anything about their care, because that is protected health information even when they brought it up first.

Positive review:

```
Thank you for taking the time to share this. Feedback like yours means a great deal to our team.
```

Mixed or negative review:

```
Thank you for the feedback. We take every comment seriously. Privacy rules prevent us from discussing any details here, but we would like to hear more. Please call our office at (305) 219-4351 and ask for the manager.
```

Suspected fake or mistaken review:

```
Thank you for the feedback. We are not able to match this to our records. If you have visited us, please call (305) 219-4351 so we can look into it.
```

Then flag it for removal in the profile if it breaks Google's policy. Never argue in public.

Phrases to avoid in any reply: "your visit", "your treatment", "your results", "when you came in", "as your provider".

## 7. Compliance reminders for anyone working this playbook

- No Meta pixel, remarketing tag or session replay on the website until counsel approves it.
- Call tracking numbers are swapped in by script only. The real number stays in the page and in every listing.
- No patient names, photos, stories or outcomes in posts, replies or listings without written authorization.
- No guarantees, no "best", no anti-aging promises, and no growth hormone marketed for performance.
- Do not name a prescription weight loss brand on the profile unless the clinic prescribes that exact product.
- Do not say "no visit needed" for testosterone or any controlled medication.
- When in doubt, leave a `[[CONFIRM]]` and ask.
